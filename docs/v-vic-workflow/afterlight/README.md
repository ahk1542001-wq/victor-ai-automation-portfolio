# AFTERLIGHT — What stays

A 49-second cinematic perfume concept film about fragrance, consideration and memory.

## Brief
Create a story-led commercial for a fictional premium perfume aimed at men who value quiet confidence and thoughtful presence. Show the fragrance inside a meaningful human moment.

## Concept
**A presence that stays.** A gentleman prepares for an evening. When his partner feels cold, he offers his blazer. Its familiar scent links his consideration with the presence she carries. The opening glimpse of her scented lapel resolves when the story returns to that moment.

## Creative direction
Charcoal tailoring, amber glass, warm interior light and cool evening backgrounds establish a consistent visual world. Close-up application shots make the product part of the preparation; the jacket gesture gives that preparation a narrative payoff. The end card returns to the bottle with “Discover the scent.”

## Storyboard
- **00:00–00:02.5 / The memory:** Open on her holding the scented lapel. The film begins with its emotional destination.
- **00:02.5–00:17 / The ritual:** Introduce the fragrance, then show preparation before wrist, nape and wool application.
- **00:17–00:27 / The meeting:** Move from his private ritual to an evening together on the terrace.
- **00:27–00:37 / The gesture:** She feels cold. He places his blazer around her shoulders, settles the fabric and releases it.
- **00:37–00:45 / The recognition:** The familiar scent connects his considerate gesture with his presence. Her glance meets his response.
- **00:45–00:49 / The invitation:** Return to the bottle and the line “A presence that stays.” Close with “Discover the scent.”
