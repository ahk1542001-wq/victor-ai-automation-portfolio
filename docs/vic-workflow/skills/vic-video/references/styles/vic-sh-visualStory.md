## Motion and edit

Every necessary action has anticipation/entrance, visible operation, result/hold and suitable exit/cut. Object movement explains the mechanism; character response makes the situation readable. Use [character motion](../runtime-map.md) for calibrated local2D actions or authorized I2V when a genuinely different action is needed. Pan/zoom alone is not acting. Preserve occlusion and contacts; reject eye/hand drift, duplicated limbs or warped faces. Don't claim full skeletal animation or phoneme sync from a2D warp/pose swap.

Use full-frame cartoon characters or the low-left VIC presenter as composition requires, without duplicate hosts. Motivate close-ups and retain spatial continuity. Supporting UI/screens/diagrams answer the story question, not an arbitrary insert quota. The real-audio proof must show the selected combination and relevant motion; main script/proof/final gates and honest review limits apply.

## Pacing and product inserts

Carry narration forward with action states, reactions, detail/result shots and motivated cuts throughout the scene. One generated cartoon sliding in and sitting unchanged under a whole paragraph is insufficient when the speech describes multiple events. Plan usable layers or distinct action states before generating art; pan/zoom, mouth movement and caption changes alone do not make the depicted story happen. Keep pauses needed for comprehension, but revise unmotivated long static holds instead of adding idle jitter or speeding speech.

For a product story, combine the cartoon situation with authentic product screens, recognizable outputs and focused feature/demo details at the relevant spoken moment; do not let a cartoon laptop replace all product evidence. Follow UI Motion's sustained-explanation/product-evidence guidance for those inserts, preserving actual source colors and provenance. Palette/background are selected for this topic, not inherited blue or the last film's cast/layout. Require a representative later-scene pacing check as well as the opening proof when their complexity differs.
